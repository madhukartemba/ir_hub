## What IR Hub Does

IR Hub brings your traditional IR-based appliances (like your TV, AC, or sound system) into your smart home. It bridges the gap between older remotes and modern automation by letting you control devices using:

- Physical button interactions on the Hub itself
- The on-device display menu
- Smart home integrations (MQTT / Home Assistant)
- Voice commands via Alexa
- Over-the-Air (OTA) firmware updates to keep your device getting better

---

## Main Menu Overview

To open the Main Menu from the Home screen, simply **long-press** the button. 

Once in the menu, you can click to navigate and long-press to select:

- **Devices**: View and manage the remotes and appliances you've already saved.
- **Add Device**: Teach the Hub new IR remote codes from your existing remotes.
- **Settings**: Adjust device preferences, check for updates, or reset the device.
- **Help**: Displays a QR code that brings you right back to this guide!
- **Contact**: Displays a QR code to email support.

---

## Settings Overview

The Settings menu lets you customize how your IR Hub behaves:

- **Sound / Haptics**: Turn device sounds and vibration feedback on or off.
- **Check for Updates**: Search for the latest firmware to keep your Hub up to date.
- **Device Info**: See your current Firmware version, the last time an update check was run, and your device's status.
- **Restart**: Safely reboot your device.
- **Reset Wi-Fi**: Clear your current Wi-Fi settings so you can connect to a new network.
- **Erase Saved Data**: Remove all saved remotes and appliances.
- **Factory Reset**: Completely wipe the device (clears Wi-Fi, saved remotes, and resets all settings) for a clean slate.

*(Tip: Keep an eye out for hidden Easter eggs!)*

## Tutorial: First-Time Setup

1. **Power On**: Plug in and power on your IR Hub.
2. **Connect to Wi-Fi**: If Wi-Fi is not configured, the device will host its own setup network. Connect to it with your phone and follow the on-screen prompts to connect the Hub to your home Wi-Fi.
3. **Customize**: Open the Main Menu (long-press), go to **Settings**, and adjust your Sound and Haptics preferences.
4. **Ready to Go**: Return to the Home screen. You are now ready to start adding your remotes!

## Tutorial: Add a Device

Want to control your TV or AC? Let's teach the Hub its commands.

1. Open the Main Menu and select **Add Device**.
2. Grab your appliance's physical remote control.
3. Point your remote directly at the IR Hub's receiver.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts on the Hub:
   - Press the button on your remote you want to record when prompted.
   - You may be asked to press it a second time to verify the signal.
5. Once successful, the device command is saved and will appear under **Devices**.

**Pro Tips for Recording:**
- Don't hold the button down for too long; a normal, firm press usually works best.
- Avoid recording in very bright, direct sunlight as it can interfere with the infrared signal.

## Tutorial: Check for Updates

Your IR Hub gets smarter over time. Here's how to ensure you have the latest software.

1. Open **Settings** -> **Check for Updates**.
2. A dedicated screen will open and automatically begin checking.
3. You will see one of the following statuses:
   - `Checking...`: Searching for new software.
   - `Up to date`: You have the latest version!
   - `Update found`: Great news! The IR Hub will automatically begin downloading and installing the new software.
   - `No Wi-Fi` or `Check failed`: See the Troubleshooting section below.

## Tutorial: Factory Reset

If you are giving your IR Hub to someone else or need a completely fresh start, use the Factory Reset option.

1. Open **Settings** -> **Factory Reset**.
2. Read the warning on the screen, then **long-press** the button to confirm.
3. The device will:
   - Forget your Wi-Fi network.
   - Erase all saved remotes and settings.
   - Restart automatically.
4. After the restart, you can set it up again from scratch.

## Troubleshooting

### Update check says `No Wi-Fi`
- Make sure your home Wi-Fi network is active.
- If you changed your router or password, use **Settings -> Reset Wi-Fi** to reconnect the Hub.

### Update check says `Check failed`
- Verify that your internet connection is working.
- Wait a few minutes and try selecting **Check for Updates** again.
